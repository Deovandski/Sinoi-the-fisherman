## Fixed requirements (from the project brief)

- Android first (phones and tablets), plus the browser version on the website. Free, no ads, no in-app purchases.
- Active play for a few minutes at a time: no idle mechanics, no background progress.
- Based on Stardew Valley's fishing, taken further. Loop: fish, sell, upgrade gear and abilities, catch harder fish, travel.
- Set in the #HusbandOutWorld world; players fish different regions; travel costs money (exact costs once the West March travel map is done).
- A random mechanic that pushes players to act, such as going somewhere else.
- No seasons, no day/night cycle.
- New fish are based on real fish, named by translating into two languages, chopping and joining.
- Pixel art like Stardew Valley.
