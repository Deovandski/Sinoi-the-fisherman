# Privacy

Sinoi the Fisherman collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, in the app or on the website.

## The game (Android app and browser version)

- Your save is stored only on your device: in the app's private storage on Android, or in the website's browser storage (IndexedDB) on the web.
- Nothing is sent to us or anyone else. The game works offline.
- When you **export** a save, the game writes a JSON file where you choose (or to your downloads, or to your clipboard). When you **import** one, it reads only the file you pick.
- The Android app asks for no permissions.
- Links to Amazon and GitHub open in your browser; those sites have their own privacy policies.

## The website

The website at https://deovandski.github.io/Sinoi-the-fisherman/ is hosted on GitHub Pages, so [GitHub](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) sees your IP address when it sends you the pages. The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

## Google Play

If you install the app from Google Play, Google handles the download and may collect data under its own policies. The app itself adds nothing.
