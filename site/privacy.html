Privacy

Sinoi the Fisherman collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, in the app or on this website.

The game (Android app and browser version)

  • Your save is stored only on your device: in the app's private storage on Android, or in this site's browser storage (IndexedDB) on the web.
  • Nothing is sent to us or anyone else. The game works offline.
  • When you export a save, the game writes a JSON file to the place you choose (or your downloads, or your clipboard). When you import one, it reads only the file you pick.
  • The Android app asks for no permissions.
  • Links to Amazon and GitHub open in your browser; those sites have their own privacy policies.

This website

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub sees your IP address when it sends you the pages. The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

Google Play

If you install the app from Google Play, Google handles the download and may collect data under its own policies. The app itself adds nothing.
