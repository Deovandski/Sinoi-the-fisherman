# AI Disclosure

This project is built with an AI assistant, **Claude** (by Anthropic), working under the direction of Deovandski Skibinski Junior. This page says plainly which parts are human work, which parts the AI wrote, and how the two fit together. It follows the same approach as the [Vraki West March Campaign's disclosure](https://github.com/Deovandski/Vraki-West-March-Campaign-D-D/blob/main/AI-DISCLOSURE.md).

## The short version

- **The world is human-made.** The #HusbandOutWorld world, its lore, kingdoms, characters and names come from Deovandski Skibinski Junior. The series illustrations are by **Mitsukiven** and **Feiourm**.
- **The game concept and design decisions are human.** Deovandski set the concept (an active, touch-first fishing game in the world of Vraki), the rules (no seasons, no day/night cycle, no idle play, free with no ads or purchases, the fish-naming method) and every design decision recorded in the white paper (the fight box, one-to-two-finger play, the shadow pad, forgiveness settings, bait and tackle as the intended counter).
- **Much of the code and writing is AI-assisted.** The research, the white paper drafts, the illustrator brief, the Godot code, the website and the build workflows were written by Claude following Deovandski's direction.
- **Placeholder art may be AI-generated; final art is human.** During prototyping, placeholder sprites may be generated with AI tools. The project's illustrator draws the final art over those templates. Placeholders are marked so they don't ship.
- **A human decides.** Deovandski directs the work, reviews it and chooses what is committed. If something here contradicts the #HusbandOutWorld series, **the series wins**: please open an issue.

## Attribution statement

> **AI system:** Claude, a large language model by Anthropic, used as an assistant with access to this repository and the #HusbandOutWorld repositories (from November 2026).

> **AI contribution:** market and technical research; drafting the white paper and the illustrator brief; writing the Godot project, tests, website and workflows; placeholder assets.
